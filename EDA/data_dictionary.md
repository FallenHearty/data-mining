# data dictionary

- Age: age in years
- Sex: M or F
- ChestPainType: type of chest pain (ATA, NAP, ASY, or TA)
- RestingBP: resting blood pressure in mm Hg
- Cholesterol: serum cholesterol in mg/dL
- FastingBS: 1 if fasting blood sugar is above 120 mg/dL; otherwise 0
- RestingECG: resting electrocardiogram result
- MaxHR: maximum heart rate achieved
- ExerciseAngina: exercise-induced angina, Y or N
- Oldpeak: exercise-related ST depression compared with rest
- ST_Slope: slope of the peak exercise ST segment (up, flat, or down)
- HeartDisease: 1 for heart disease; 0 for no heart disease

## chest pain codes

- ATA: atypical angina
- NAP: non-anginal pain
- ASY: asymptomatic
- TA: typical angina
